🌿 Green Tips

Simple habits can make a big difference to our planet.

💡 Save Electricity

Switch off lights, fans and electronic devices when they are not being used.

💧 Save Water

Close the tap while brushing and repair leaking taps.

🛍️ Avoid Plastic

Carry reusable bags and avoid single-use plastic.

🌳 Plant Trees

Plant and protect trees to create a healthier environment.
